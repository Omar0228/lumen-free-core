THE SOVEREIGN CHAIN

How your question travels

  1. SYN — Receive: Lumen receives your question.
  2. SYN-ACK — Route: Match the question to a reviewed lesson and identify its topic.
  3. Scripture Wall: Attach a relevant Bible reference, carefully distinguished from technical evidence.
  4. Verify: Explain only what the reviewed lesson supports; mark gaps clearly.
  5. ACK — Return: Present BLUE, GREEN, GOLD, PURPLE and a teach-back challenge.

SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK is a teaching analogy inspired by TCP's three-way handshake, not an actual network protocol or guarantee of delivery. This local prototype does not run a general-purpose AI model.

Privacy and family sharing

Your typed question is processed in your browser. This website has no sign-in, analytics, server-side question storage, or paid AI connection. Answers are not sent to the site owner by this code. Your hosting provider may still collect standard web access logs. Do not enter private information. Voice input is intentionally omitted because browser speech services can transmit audio to third parties.

Share the published URL with family members. They will see the same public lesson library, not your private ChatGPT conversations, memories, or unpublished commands.
