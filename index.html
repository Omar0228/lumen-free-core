THE SOVEREIGN CHAIN

How your question travels

  1. SYN — Receive: Lumen receives your question.
  2. SYN-ACK — Route: Match the question to a reviewed lesson and identify its topic.
  3. Scripture Wall: Display a relevant Bible reference when supported by the reviewed lesson, distinguished from technical evidence.
  4. Verify: Explain only what the reviewed lesson supports; mark gaps clearly.
  5. ACK — Return: Present BLUE, GREEN, GOLD, PURPLE and a teach-back challenge.

SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK is a teaching analogy inspired by TCP's three-way handshake. Experimental AI answers are separate from reviewed lessons and are not automatically verified.
